Admission and Enquiries

Where is MARKAZ located?

MARKAZ is located at Compensation Layout / Old Kuutunku, Gwagwalada, Abuja, Nigeria. You can use the Google Maps link in the page footer for directions.

How can I enquire about admission?

Contact the school by email or phone using the details on our Contact Us page. The school can guide you through the current admission process.

Which documents or requirements do I need?

Admission requirements may depend on the class and the student's circumstances. Please contact the school before applying so you can obtain the current requirements and any necessary documents.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Please contact the school to enquire about arranging a visit and to confirm a suitable time.

What age range does MARKAZ admit?

MARKAZ accepts students aged 3 to 10 years. Please contact the school to confirm class placement and current admission requirements.

Classes and Learning

Does MARKAZ focus on Qur’an memorisation?

Yes. Qur’an memorisation is a central priority at MARKAZ, alongside academic learning and character development.

Does the school provide academic education too?

MARKAZ supports Qur’an learning alongside academic education. Visit the Academics page or contact the school for information about current classes and subjects.

How can I find out which class is suitable for my child?

Contact the school with your child's age, previous schooling and learning background. The school can advise you on the appropriate class and any assessment needed.

Which classes does MARKAZ offer?

MARKAZ provides classes from KG through JSS 3. Contact the school for guidance on class placement and the subjects offered at each level.

Assessments and Attendance

How are students' academic results assessed?

Students' academic performance is recorded through assessment and examination results. For details about the current assessment arrangements, please contact the school.

How important is regular attendance?

Regular attendance helps students follow lessons, maintain revision routines and make consistent progress in both academic learning and Qur’an memorisation.

What should I do if my child will be absent?

Please notify the school and follow its attendance procedure. Contact the school directly if you need to confirm the appropriate process.

Facilities and Student Life

What are the school's hours?

The school's stated hours are from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

Which days does the school operate?

The school operates from Saturday through Thursday. Friday is the weekly rest day.

Does MARKAZ offer boarding and day-student options?

MARKAZ offers both boarding and day-student options. Please contact the school to confirm current availability, accommodation arrangements and requirements.

How can I learn more about the school's facilities?

Visit the Facilities page for an overview of the learning environment, or contact the school for more information about student arrangements.

Contact and Support

How can I contact MARKAZ?

Email markazilquran@gmail.com or use one of the telephone numbers listed in the page footer. You can also visit the Contact Us page for the available contact options.

Where can I find more information about the school?

Visit the About, Academics, Admissions and Facilities pages using the main navigation. If something is not covered, please contact the school for clarification.
