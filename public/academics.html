THE TAHFIZ JOURNEY

Progressing Towards Complete Memorisation تحفيظ القرآن

Our Arabia pathway is organised into stages that guide learners from their introduction to memorisation through progressive learning and revision.

01

Foundation Stage

Learners build confidence in Qur’an recitation and begin memorising shorter passages with guidance.

02

Memorisation Stage

Learners progress through structured memorisation stages while revising earlier portions to strengthen accuracy and retention.

03

Qur’an Completion Stage

Learners work towards memorising the complete Glorious Qur’an, with regular revision to retain what they have learned.

Progress depends on each learner's pace, consistency and memorisation development.
