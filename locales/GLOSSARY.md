## Translation boundaries

- Translate interface labels, instructions, buttons, headings, status labels, accessible names and placeholders.
- Never translate student names, admission numbers, teacher names, email addresses, phone numbers, user-entered remarks, class/subject records, or any other database-derived value.
- Keep Arabic UI right-to-left. Keep numeric identifiers and phone numbers legible in their natural order.
- Do not use runtime machine translation. Add reviewed entries to the locale dictionary.
